# Storylines

Chosen: **One question on the map.** A researcher asks whether one month was
unusual. The film follows one AOI into the actual calendar, a selected month,
the calibration comparison and a reusable CSV. A continuous visual handoff
keeps the same region and month legible; measured figures arrive only when
supported by the finished archive. Ground: the app's dark slate, cyan selections,
large white explanatory text. Sound: quiet minimal instrumental if an authorized
catalog track is available; otherwise a silent jury film with readable text.

Alternative: **The sensor on trial.** A split comparison of native and calibrated
observations becomes a paired-sensor investigation, with held-out data as the
last witness. More abstract and less direct about the user's workflow.

Alternative: **A month travels through time.** One calendar column expands into
its historical baseline, then contracts back into the regional map. A stronger
visual conceit, but risks hiding coverage and encouraging a causal interpretation
of a temporal ratio.
